VOXELON · Build plan · RPG mode

The Summit Descent

A second world beside Warfare: one shared 1500² map with a mountain at the centre. You spawn on the peak and work downward — four skills, four levelable spawners on a private plot, and instanced boss lairs holding the encounters the engine already runs. Roughly 70% of this ships on systems that are already in the repo.

1  The split

The existing game becomes a named mode rather than the only thing behind the Play button.

Warfare is the current 5000² faction world: territory, machines, vehicles, silos, seasons, the boss-powered tech tree. The word is already the right one — src/warfare.ts and src/warfare_ui.ts own it, and “Warfare Command” (G) becomes that mode's progression screen instead of an oddly-named subsystem inside a mode with no name.

RPG is new. The Arena (Duels, Bedwars, CTF) is untouched.

On the title screen, #play-btn stops dropping straight into the faction world and opens a two-card mode picker above the existing Minigames button. Everything else on that screen — auth, character preview, the Arena modal — stays exactly where it is.

Rename surface

  • Title copy in index.html (subtitle, .title-facts) moves onto the Warfare card.
  • The pause menu, world map and HUD gain a mode label.
  • No module renames. warfare.ts keeps its name and meaning; it simply becomes mode-scoped.

2  What already exists

The build is small because the content isn't. This is the honest ledger of what gets reused versus what gets written.

Reused as-is

  • vault_encounter.ts3-phase BossDefinition, six hazard shapes, add-waves, sealed arenas, contribution tracking
  • vaults.tsFive finished bosses — Bone Warden, Mire Queen, Ember Colossus, Crystal Seer, Gilded Artificer
  • boss_music.tsPer-family soundtracks with phase transitions, driven by audio.startVaultMusic
  • pvp_feedback.tsDamageNumbers, KillBanner, StreakTracker — documented as reusable outside Duels
  • terrain.tsmountainFactor, rock line y=96, snow line y=120, peaks to y≈235
  • runes.tsSocketed affixes: armor, toughness, speed, mine, spread
  • mobs.tsFive mob types incl. brute; Block.MobSpawner proximity spawning
  • duels.tsThe instancing precedent: bounded arena, server-pushed entry, clamped movement
  • warfare_ui.tsBranching tree renderer to reskin for the skill panel
  • accounts.tsExplicit per-mode progression blobs, kept out of the client-owned data

Written new

  • rpg.tsPure: skill XP curves, level tables, automatic unlocks, area gates, sanitize/migrate
  • rpg_spawners.tsPure: spawner types, levels I–X, rates, storage caps, upgrade costs
  • rpg_shop.tsPure: four vendor catalogues, coin prices, level requirements
  • rpg_world.tsArea bounds, summit spawn, gate checks, plot allocation
  • feel.tsThe universal feedback layer — vacuum, XP splash, combo meter, level-up slam
  • crops.tsCrop blocks and growth stages (nothing farming-shaped exists today)
  • rpg_ui.tsSkill panel, shop, plot manager, area cards

The architectural rule this inherits

Every new pure module follows warfare.ts and duels_progression.ts: no THREE, no DOM, no Node. The authoritative server and the client run the identical numbers, so a spawner tick or a skill level means the same thing everywhere. Protocol changes stay additive on ClientMsg/ServerMsg.

3  The four skills

Levels 1–50 each. Every level grants a small passive automatically; every fifth level grants a real unlock, also automatically — nothing to equip, nothing to choose. Skill Average is the mean of the four and gates the late areas, so nobody reaches the end on Combat alone.

SkillXP fromPer levelThe AoE unlockFeeds
Combat Kills, weighted by mob tier Crit chance & damage Cleave — one swing hits the whole pack Access to high-tier resource areas
Mining Ore and stone broken Mine speed Vein Miner — the whole vein in one hit Every weapon and tool tier
Foraging Logs chopped, by wood type Extra log drops Treefeller — the entire tree falls Wands and rune catalysts
Farming Crops harvested Max health Field Sweep — 3×3 → 5×5 → full row, self-replanting Food buffs and potions — boss prep

The cross-dependency column is the load-bearing one. Mining gates gear, Farming gates survival, Foraging gates magic, Combat gates access. A player who grinds one hits a wall and has to go play another — that pressure is what stops four parallel bars from feeling like four chores.

The engine reality per skill

  • Mining has a ready-made 7-step ladder: Coal → Iron → Gold → Redstone → Diamond → Titanium → Cobalt, with per-block hardness and tool tiers already in blocks.ts.
  • Foraging has five wood types (Oak, Birch, Spruce, Jungle, Cherry). Rarer woods live behind gates and are the magic supply line.
  • Combat has Item.Sword, already mobs-only because PvP in Warfare is guns-only — a melee world is the path of least resistance.
  • Farming has nothing. Crops are the single largest net-new content lift in the mode.

Melee and magic

Guns are removed from the RPG world entirely — the cleanest way to make the two modes feel like different games, and it's a subtraction rather than a build. Melee runs on sword tiers off Mining, with a charged heavy swing. Magic runs on wands with mana, built on runes.ts: a wand fires an element (ember / frost / prism / venom) that maps onto the existing boss families. The build axis is melee ↔ magic ↔ hybrid, expressed through gear, never through a class picked at character creation.

4  Plots and spawners

The second pillar. Every player gets a private walled plot off the Summit hub — the only place in the mode where blocks can be placed. Friends can visit.

SpawnerProducesServesBreakpoints (III / VI / X)
Mob Spawner Mob drops → coinsCombat Zombie → Spitter → Brute
Ore Node Ore, by tierMining Iron → Diamond → Titanium
Grove Logs, by wood typeForaging Oak → Spruce → Cherry
Crop Bed Crops → food and potion matsFarming Wheat → Gourd → Emberroot

Each level improves rate, yield and storage cap; the three breakpoints change the output outright, so upgrading has a visible payoff rather than a number crawling upward. Output autocollects into the spawner's storage — no standing there swinging.

Three rules that keep this from eating the game

  1. Spawners give resources and coins — never skill XP. If a spawner levels Combat, the skill system becomes an idle bar and the world empties out. Spawners fund you; playing levels you. This is the single most important balance call in the mode.
  2. They tick whenever you are online, anywhere in the world — not only on the plot. Online-only accrual with autocollect would otherwise reward standing on your plot watching numbers. This way you play the Quarry while the plot fills, and drop by to empty it. Plots reward being logged in, not being AFK.
  3. Placement capacity grows with Skill Average — roughly 4 slots at the start, ~15 at the end. Plot capacity becomes a progression reward, and the plot never becomes an infinite factory.

Upgrade costs are paid in the spawner's own output plus something from a different skill, so plots enforce the cross-dependency instead of letting each line self-fund. Storage borrows the chests.ts pattern: server is the source of truth, client keeps a display cache.

Death, with zero coins dropped

Death currently costs nothing at all, which is a defensible call for a kids' game — but it also means dying to a boss reads as nothing happening. The plan assumes one soft cost that needs no new systems: you lose your combo multiplier and get run back to the last shrine. Felt immediately, permanent damage nowhere. Say the word and it comes out.

5  The shop

Four vendors at the Summit, roughly six items each — every one unlocked by a skill level and bought with coins. The shop is the skill reward screen.

VendorStock
CombatThree sword tiers · two wands (ember, frost) · one armour set
MiningThree pickaxe tiers · the Drill (3×3 continuous) · two mining consumables
ForagingThree axe tiers · the Treefeller axe · two rune catalysts
FarmingThree hoe tiers · seed packs · two food and potion recipes

Plus the spawner shop: buy spawner types, buy upgrade levels, buy plot capacity. Deliberately absent — rarities, random stat rolls, an auction house. Every item is a known, named thing a kid can save toward. Runes stay as the customisation layer on top.

6  Areas and bosses

A mountain at the centre of ~1500². Everything is a walk down and outward from a landmark visible from anywhere, so nobody gets lost.

AreaGateBossHolds
The Summit—— Spawn, four vendors, bank, skill shrine, plot portal. Safe.
Pine Slopesnonemini-boss First trees, first mobs, first crop bed. The tutorial in terrain form.
The QuarryMining 5 · hardBone Warden Exposed veins, crypt mobs, the first real gear tier.
Sunken MireFarming 8 · hardMire Queen Wet farmland, potion crops, the consumable economy opens.
AshfellCombat 15 · softEmber Colossus Door is open; the mobs will flatten you. Titanium and rare wood.

Hard gates are doors you physically cannot pass — used only where skipping would break progression. Soft gates are level pressure: walk in and die, if you like. Ashfell is deliberately soft so brave players can push.

Instanced lairs

The overworld is public; boss lairs are not. You walk into the door with your party and the server hands back a private copy of the fight — the same shape as net.onDuelArena, which already ships a bounded arena, a spawn point and clamped movement. Respawn becomes trivial: the instance closes, the next party gets a fresh boss. Party XP credit is already solved by the contribution rules in warfare.ts (qualifiesForWarfareXp, damage and time fractions), which explicitly keep credit for players who died before the kill.

Shared-world resources

Ore veins, trees and crops in the public world need timed regrowth so one player can't strip an area — VAULT_RECHARGE's 30-minute regrow already establishes that pattern.

The widening pass — Prism Hollows / Crystal Seer, Clockwork Foundry / Gilded Artificer, and an endgame boss inside the mountain you spawned on, opened once all five are down. You climb back up to where you started for the last door. Build the area and gate systems as data tables from day one so this is content, not a refactor.

7  The feel layer

Built once, skinned four times. This is what decides whether the mode is loved or merely finished, so it lands early — before the content that depends on it.

The unifying rule: every skill gets a moment where one action produces many results. A whole tree, a whole vein, a whole field, a whole pack. It is the difference between doing a chore forty times and erasing a forest. Each is gated as that skill's mid-tier tool, and the tools below it are tuned to feel deliberately hungry for it.

Six shared systems in feel.ts

  1. The vacuum. Drops fly to the player. Never make anyone walk over forty items. The single biggest satisfaction upgrade in the mode.
  2. XP splash. Every action pops a floating +12 Foraging and fills a bar segment with a click that pitches up as you chain.
  3. Combo meter. Chain actions inside a window → multiplier tiers, each with its own colour, sound and screen-edge glow. One system, four palettes.
  4. The level-up slam. Full-screen banner, a chord, a pillar of light — and the unlock card shown right there: MINING 10 — VEIN MINER UNLOCKED. Never make them open a menu to find out what they won.
  5. Milestone tiers at 5 / 10 / 25 / 50 get a louder version: fireworks, a title, a nameplate tag other players can see.
  6. Escalating pitch on every repeated action, reset after a pause. Nearly free, and it is most of why chaining feels good.

Per-skill staging

  • Foraging — the tree topples rather than blinking out: trunk tips, canopy shudders, a low rumble. Chops pitch up, then a crack and a timber whoosh. Leaves burst, logs erupt and vacuum in, a sapling auto-plants in the stump. Chain trees for LUMBER RUSH ×2 ×3 ×4.
  • Mining — the whole vein detonates and arcs toward you; ten diamonds at once is a jackpot, ten swings is a job. Ore-sense makes nearby veins glow faintly through stone, because the joy of mining is finding. The Drill carves a continuous 3×3 with shake, sparks and a rising whine, slightly out of control.
  • Farming — harvest ripples outward from you as a visible wave, replanting behind itself. Golden sweep, a crunchy snap, and the fastest XP tick in the game. It should feel like mowing a perfect lawn.
  • Combat — hit-stop of 50–80ms on heavy hits, mobs bursting into scattering voxel chunks instead of a flat poof, an execute finisher under 20% health. DamageNumbers and KillBanner already exist and only need pointing at mobs; StreakTracker hands Combat a full announcer vocabulary for free.

Two constraints to respect from the first line of code

Particle budget. particles.ts caps at 250 particles of 0.18-unit planes — sized for death poofs, not for a whole tree exploding. That budget needs raising and probably an instanced or pooled path, or the fifth tree in a Lumber Rush will look weaker than the first.

Accessibility. index.html already carries comfort modes that strip motion and a photosensitivity mode that removes strobing particles outright. Every shake, hit-stop, flash and vignette here routes through those switches from the start — retrofitting it later is miserable.

8  Build order

Phase by phase, each one green before the next starts: npx tsc --noEmit clean, smoke tests for new pure logic, noUnusedLocals means unused imports are hard errors. Source has multibyte characters, so grep with -a.

1

Mode split

index.html · main.ts · protocol.ts · server_core.ts

Introduce a mode concept and a two-card picker behind #play-btn. The existing world becomes Warfare with no behavioural change. RPG is a stub realm that loads an empty bounded world.

Done when Warfare plays exactly as it does today, reached through one extra click, and RPG loads without errors.

2

The realm and the summit

rpg_world.ts · terrain.ts · server_core.ts

A 1500² bounded world with a central peak, seeded separately from Warfare. Spawn on the summit. Faction, territory, machine, vehicle, silo, turret and gun systems are all switched off — RPG opts in, it does not opt out.

Done when you spawn on a peak, can walk down in any direction, and none of Warfare's systems are reachable.

3

Skills, XP and persistence

rpg.ts (new, pure) · accounts.ts · protocol.ts

Four skills, XP curves, level tables, automatic unlock application, and an rpg blob on Account stored explicitly beside warfare and duelProgress — never inside the client-owned data, so a state save can't mint levels. XP awards are server-authoritative with an offline parity path.

Done when chopping, mining, killing and harvesting each move the right bar, and levels survive a relog.

4

The feel layer

feel.ts (new) · particles.ts · audio.ts · pvp_feedback.ts

All six shared systems, plus the particle budget raise and the accessibility routing. Deliberately ahead of the content: every later phase plugs into this rather than growing its own feedback.

Done when a single ore break already feels good with no AoE tool involved, and comfort modes visibly quiet it.

5

Farming from nothing

crops.ts (new) · blocks.ts · textures.ts

Crop blocks as shape: 'cross' billboards on the existing plant() helper, with growth stages, seeds, and procedural tile art in the same style as paintTallGrass. The largest net-new content lift in the mode, so it lands before the systems that depend on it.

Done when a crop plants, grows through visible stages, harvests, and feeds the Farming bar.

6

The AoE verbs

rpg.ts · main.ts · feel.ts · world.ts

Treefeller, Vein Miner, Field Sweep and Cleave — each server-validated (a multi-block break is one authoritative action, not a burst of client edits) and each staged through the feel layer.

Done when one swing fells a tree, one hit clears a vein, and both feel better than four swings did.

7

Plots, spawners, coins and the shop

rpg_spawners.ts · rpg_shop.ts (both new, pure) · chests.ts · rpg_ui.ts

Private plots as the only buildable space, four spawner types with levels I–X, autocollect into server-owned storage, world-wide online ticking, capacity from Skill Average — then coins and the four vendors to spend them at.

Done when a spawner fills while you play elsewhere, empties into your inventory, and pays for the next tool tier.

8

Areas, gates and instanced bosses

rpg_world.ts · vault_encounter.ts · server_core.ts

Five areas as data, hard and soft gates, resource regrowth, and lair doors that hand a party its own encounter instance on the onDuelArena pattern. Three bosses wired: Bone Warden, Mire Queen, Ember Colossus — unchanged encounters, new staging.

Done when two parties can fight the same boss simultaneously without seeing each other, and both get credit.

9

Balance and the loop

rpg.ts tuning tables · smoke tests

Tune the full session: collect, sell, upgrade, descend, level, clear, return. Verify that no single skill can carry a player to the end, and that spawner income never outpaces active play.

Done when a fresh account can reach the Bone Warden in one sitting and wants a second.

9  Known risks

  • Spawner income outrunning play The failure mode is a plot that pays better than the world. Keep spawners on resources and coins only, never skill XP, and tune income to trail active play at every tier.
  • Farming as the weak fourth skill It is the only skill built from scratch and the easiest to ship thin. Its cross-dependency — no food, no boss — has to be real, or players will skip it and hit the Sunken Mire gate resenting it.
  • Particle and audio budget Four AoE verbs firing into a 250-particle cap will visibly starve during combos. Raise it in phase 4, before anything depends on how it looks.
  • Shared-world resource stripping A public 1500² map is small. Regrowth timers need tuning against real concurrency, and per-player node instancing is the fallback if timers aren't enough.
  • Mode bleed Warfare and RPG must not share progression, gear or currency. The account blobs are separate by design; the discipline is keeping every new system mode-scoped from the first commit rather than retrofitting a split later.
