Parkour

Eight worlds, one straight line. Fifty-six jumps run dead ahead: stepping stones, narrow beams, doorway walls, covered tunnels, weaving slaloms, gaps punched through the pad, hurdles, arches, long drops and climbs — never a maze. Two fortresses, one span between them — a single block wide, the whole way. Fight for it with an iron axe and a bow, repair it with infinite team wool, and dive into the enemy portal to score. Knock your rival off and they start again at their own base. First to five goals takes it.
